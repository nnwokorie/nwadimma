MGT 409 · Final project · Nkechi Nwokorie

Itoju 🩺

A four-agent copilot that tells Nigeria's community health workers — in Pidgin or English — whether a sick child must be referred now, with every dose taken from the WHO protocol, never invented by the AI.

github.com/nnwokorie/itoju · gpt-6-luna via Portkey

01 The problem

  • A CHEW is often the only trained health worker a rural family sees.
  • In minutes: danger sign? which drug? how many tablets?
  • Notes come in English, Pidgin, Hausa, Yoruba. The paper iCCM booklet is slow under pressure.

08 Limits & what's next

Limits today

  • Not clinically validated — 14 written cases
  • Needs internet
  • iCCM 2–59 months only
  • Straight-line distance; ~25% facilities "unknown" status

Next

  • Pilot with a state PHC board: 200 real cases vs. nurses
  • WhatsApp front end + offline iCCM engine
  • Hausa / Yoruba voice notes
  • Day-3 follow-up agent + supervisor dashboard
