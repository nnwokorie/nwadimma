Maternal emergencies · Nigeria

Getting mothers to the right hospital, in time.

Nigeria loses about 993 mothers for every 100,000 live births. Most die of severe bleeding or eclampsia, which are treatable if she reaches a hospital that can operate and give blood. Nwa Dị Mma helps the person beside her decide fast and go to the right place.

Why this matters

Most maternal deaths are not a mystery. The causes are known and treatable. Women die because help comes too late. Global health calls this the three delays.

≈29%
of global maternal deaths are in Nigeria

The highest share of any country. About 993 deaths per 100,000 live births in 2023, versus about 19 in the United States (CDC, 2023).

4
causes behind most deaths

Severe bleeding, pre-eclampsia and eclampsia, infection, and obstructed labour. Each has clear warning signs a trained helper can spot.
